# Vietnam Biofuel Atlas — Design Specification (Field Atlas)

## 1. Aesthetic & Identity Philosophy

The **Vietnam Biofuel Atlas** is an editorial, data-rich stakeholder decision tool part of the **AI4U.now** ecosystem. The visual language follows **Contemporary Editorial Cartography (Field Atlas)**: tactile, place-aware, grounded, and built for policy, research, and investment dialogues.

### Core Principles
- **Evidence-First Hierarchy:** High data density balanced with scannable metrics, structured disclosure, and clear methodological qualifications.
- **Agricultural & Editorial Materiality:** Muted indigo ink backgrounds, tactile warm parchment accents, and rice-straw gold highlights without neon or generic tech clichés.
- **Ecosystem Consistency:** Integrated official **AI4U.now** gold lightning wordmark and branding, aligning with `ai4u.now` standards.

---

## 2. Color Palette & Tokens

### Primary & Background
- **Deep Indigo Ink (Base):** `#111b28` / `hsl(217, 39%, 11%)` — Primary background and container canvas.
- **Deep Canvas Dark:** `#0c141f` / `hsl(216, 43%, 8%)` — Outer page background and navigation rail.
- **Parchment White (Ink Light):** `#f3f4f6` — High-contrast primary text.
- **Muted Parchment:** `#9ba8b8` — Body text and secondary notes.

### Signature Highlight & AI4U Brand
- **Rice-Straw Gold / AI4U Gold:** `#e3a72f` / `hsl(39, 78%, 54%)` — Brand mark, active states, key data bars, and prominent badges.
- **Gold Hover / Accent:** `#f0bc4d` — Hover states and focused controls.

### Semantic Feedstock Colors
- **Rice System (Husk & Straw):** `#e3a72f` (Gold) / `#d4a344` (Straw)
- **Sugar System (Bagasse):** `#7d9d68` (Cane Green)
- **Cassava System (Roots/Ethanol):** `#c76d43` (Clay Terracotta)
- **Livestock System (Manure/Biogas):** `#466d5b` (Mineral Forest Green)
- **Regional Residues (Coffee & Coconut):** `#8a6844` (Husk Brown)

---

## 3. Typography

- **Headings & Major Numerals:** `DM Serif Display`, serif, with tight line height and editorial authority.
- **Body, UI Controls & Labels:** `Manrope`, sans-serif, high legibility across data displays and mobile views.
- **Microcopy & Field Stamps:** Uppercase tracked labels (`letter-spacing: 0.08em; font-size: 0.75rem`).

---

## 4. Components & Structural Layout

- **Left Rail Navigation:** Persistent index rail on desktop (`240px`), collapsible drawer on mobile.
- **Hero Field Sheet:** Asymmetric hero section combining deep indigo imagery overlay with high-contrast editorial typography and field notes.
- **Data Cards & Feedstock Grid:** Clean border styling (`rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`), dynamic progress indicators, and prominent risk qualifiers.
- **Interactive E10 Scenario Planner:** Real-time slider and calculated metrics for national blending gap analysis.
- **Ecosystem Footer:** AI4U.now ecosystem brand mark, research citations, and navigation links.
